A library for your Linux games.

Estante brings Steam, Faugus, Heroic and local game launchers together in one interface. Browse your collection with a gamepad, keyboard or mouse, and choose from four layouts.

The project is in development and built with Python and Qt Quick/PySide6. It includes categories, search, custom cover artwork and playtime tracking.
