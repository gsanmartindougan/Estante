

[01-presentacion]

A library for your Linux games.

Estante brings Steam, Faugus, Heroic and local game launchers together in one interface. Browse your collection with a gamepad, keyboard or mouse, and choose from four layouts.

The project is in development and built with Python and Qt Quick/PySide6. It includes categories, search, custom cover artwork and playtime tracking.

Code and documentation:
https://github.com/gsanmartindougan/Estante

[02-apariencias]

Four layouts for the same collection.

Estante lets you choose how you browse your library:

• List: a logo wheel alongside the game details.
• Grid: organized covers with an information panel on the side.
• Carousel: centered selection and circular navigation.
• Sense: a compact row over the game background.

The video shows all four views. Covers, backgrounds and selection can also be adjusted for each layout.

[03-origenes]

Your collection can come from different places.

Estante can import games from Steam, Faugus and Heroic through Settings. You can also add local .sh scripts and AppImages, with their launch arguments and working directory.

Games managed by other launchers continue to use their launch configuration. Those launchers must be installed to run their games.

Project and documentation:
